I'm Aritejh (he/him), and I'm based in Singapore. I work at Visa on AI agents and the infrastructure for discovering, hosting, and deploying them.

## Outside work

I'm building **Courtly SG**. It takes badminton listings posted on Telegram and puts them on a searchable map, with filters for date, time, and location, so it's easier to see which sessions are available.

## With Women Devs SG

I help out as a volunteer and ally, through code and talks.
